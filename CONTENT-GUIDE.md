# Content and Launch Checklist

## Before going live

- Confirm exact Google Maps pin and replace the search-based map link if needed.
- Confirm shop opening hours.
- Add 8–15 genuine garment photographs.
- Add real customer testimonials only after approval.
- Confirm final domain name and update `.env.local`.
- Add Instagram/Facebook links when available.

## Recommended real photo list

1. Shopfront landscape photograph
2. Tailor taking measurements
3. Close-up of lapel and button finishing
4. Two pant-coat looks
5. Two kurta-pajama looks
6. Two wedding or sherwani looks
7. Fabric shelves or draping detail
8. Owner/team portrait

## Easy future upgrades

- Database-backed admin panel

- Order-status tracking
- Punjabi/Hindi language switch
